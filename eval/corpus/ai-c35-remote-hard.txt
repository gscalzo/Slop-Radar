Four years of hiring across eight time zones, and the thing that mattered most was not tooling.

It was writing an actual rule about response time and then defending it. Ours was: nothing is expected within the working day of another zone, and anything genuinely urgent goes through a paging channel with a named on-call.

Without that rule, people invent their own, and the invented ones are always stricter. Someone in Europe sees a message from San Francisco at 7pm and answers, because the alternative is being the person who didn't. Within a few months the effective policy is that everyone is available always, and nobody decided it.

Async doesn't happen because you bought the tools. It happens because someone specified what waiting looks like.
