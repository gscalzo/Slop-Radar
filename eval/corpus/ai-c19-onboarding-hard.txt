We measured how long it took new engineers to merge their first change. It was eleven days.

Most of that wasn't learning the codebase. It was access. Waiting on a VPN ticket, then a repo permission, then a staging credential that required someone on holiday to approve.

We built a checklist that ran on the offer acceptance rather than the start date, and moved every approval to a single owner. First merge dropped to under two days.

The interesting part was the secondary effect. People who shipped something in week one asked more questions in week two. The ones who spent a fortnight waiting had already learned that the way to get things done here was to sit quietly and wait for someone else.

Onboarding teaches behaviour, not just process.
