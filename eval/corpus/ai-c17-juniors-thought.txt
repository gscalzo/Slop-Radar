Everyone is hiring senior engineers. Almost nobody is training junior ones.

This is not a talent shortage. It is a coordination failure. Every company individually decides that training is too expensive and someone else's problem, and collectively the industry stops producing the seniors it plans to hire in five years.

We took ten juniors last year. It cost us real senior time — roughly a day a week each for the first three months. Two didn't work out. Eight are now shipping independently, and three are better than people we hired at mid-level.

The maths works. It just doesn't work inside a single quarter, which is the only unit most companies can think in.

If you can afford to train, train. The people you'll want to hire in 2029 are being turned away right now.
