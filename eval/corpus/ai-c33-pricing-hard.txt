We raised prices 30% and lost 4% of customers. I had expected to lose 15%.

The interesting part was who left. Almost entirely from the smallest tier, mostly accounts that had never contacted support and were using one feature. The customers I'd been anxious about — the loud ones, the ones with strong opinions in the community — stayed without much comment.

What I'd underestimated was that price anxiety and price sensitivity are different things. The loud accounts were engaged, and engagement is not the same as being marginal. The quiet ones on the cheap tier had been marginal all along.

If you're putting off a price change because of a specific customer you're afraid of losing, check whether that customer is actually price sensitive or just vocal.
