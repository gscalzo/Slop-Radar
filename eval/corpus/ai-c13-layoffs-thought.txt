If you are laying people off this quarter, three things matter more than your severance package.

Tell them first. Not the all-hands, not the press release, not a calendar invite titled "quick sync" at 8am. The person losing their job should never learn it from a room.

Be specific about why. "Restructuring" tells someone nothing they can use. If the role is gone, say the role is gone. If the bet didn't work, say the bet didn't work. Ambiguity is where self-blame grows.

Then do the work afterwards. Write the references. Make the introductions. Answer the messages six weeks later when the initial wave of support has moved on and they're still looking.

How you exit people is watched closely by everyone who stays.
