Your strategy isn't a strategy.

It's a list of goals with adjectives.

"Become the leading platform." That's an ambition. "Grow revenue 40%." That's a target. "Delight our customers." That's a poster.

Strategy is what you're not doing.

It's the segment you're walking away from. The feature you're refusing to build. The customer you're happy to lose.

If your strategy document contains no sacrifices, you don't have a strategy. You have a wish list with a budget attached.

Real strategy is uncomfortable to write and unpopular to present. That's how you know it's working.

What's the hardest thing your company decided not to do?
