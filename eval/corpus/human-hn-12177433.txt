I write a lot while reading. Sort of like having a conversation with the author.I read less books this way, but the knowledge in the books become much more internalized. I tend to implement them much faster too. And aren&#x27;t we reading, in part, to better our lives (short-term pleasure or long-term changes)?Of course, I read fiction just for the pleasure as well (like Ready Player One).This method of reading also sharpened my bullshit detector. If I am not writing a lot, this indicates lack of insights. Either a bad book or a bad time for that book. So either way, a bad book for me.
