A thing that surprised me about running a support function: the volume of tickets is a lagging indicator of product decisions made nine months earlier.

We could predict the next quarter's contact rate from the previous quarter's release notes with reasonable accuracy. Every ambiguous button, every silent failure, every settings page that needed a paragraph of explanation showed up eventually as human hours.

Support was being measured on response time. Nobody was measuring the thing generating the tickets.

We started putting the top ten contact drivers in front of the product team monthly, framed as cost. Not complaints — cost. Hours, salaries, a number.

Contact rate fell 22% over three quarters, entirely from product changes. The support team barely changed anything about how they worked.
