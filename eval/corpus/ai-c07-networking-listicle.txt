🤝 Networking advice that actually works for introverts:

1️⃣ Skip the mixer. Volunteer at the event instead — you get a role, a script, and a reason to talk to everyone.
2️⃣ Follow up within 24 hours or don't bother. Memory is the whole asset.
3️⃣ Ask about problems, not job titles. "What's hard right now?" opens more doors than "What do you do?"
4️⃣ Keep a list. Twenty warm relationships beat two thousand connections.
5️⃣ Give first. Introductions, articles, feedback. Reciprocity is a law, not a strategy.

The best networkers I know aren't charismatic. They're consistent.

Your network isn't who you met. It's who remembers you.
