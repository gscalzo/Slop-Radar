On salary negotiation, from someone who has now been on both sides of the table a few hundred times.

The single highest-leverage move is not a technique. It is knowing the band. Almost every company has one, most will tell you if asked directly, and candidates who know it negotiate within reality while candidates who don't tend to anchor either far too low or somewhere that ends the conversation.

The second is understanding what is actually flexible. Base is usually the most constrained number in the offer and the one everyone fixates on. Sign-on, start date, level, and equity refresh are frequently easier to move, and level is the one that compounds for years.

Ask what has flexibility. Most recruiters will tell you, because it makes their job easier too.
