Back from FOSDEM. Three things I'm still thinking about:

Rich Hickey's old point about incidental complexity came up in about four different talks, none of which cited him. Ideas escape their authors.

The Nix room was packed to the point people were sitting in the corridor. Whatever you think of it, that community has momentum.

Best talk I saw was from a maintainer of a library I've used for eight years and never once thought about. She's doing it alone, unpaid, for roughly 40,000 dependent repos. I sponsored her on the train home. You should probably check who's holding up your stack.
