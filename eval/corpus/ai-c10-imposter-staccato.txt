Imposter syndrome.

Everyone talks about it. Nobody solves it.

Here's why.

We treat it as a confidence problem. It isn't. It's an information problem.

You feel like a fraud because you're comparing your inside to everyone else's outside. Your doubt against their highlight reel. Your process against their polished output.

The fix isn't affirmations. It's evidence.

Keep a file. Every win. Every thank you. Every problem you solved that nobody else could.

Read it when the voice starts.

Feelings aren't facts. But receipts are.
