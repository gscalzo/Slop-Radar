Culture is not your values statement. It is not your offsite. It is not the fruit in the kitchen or the slogan on the wall.

Culture is what happens when a senior person behaves badly and nothing occurs.

Every organisation teaches its people what is actually rewarded, and it teaches this far more effectively through exceptions than through policy. One tolerated bully undoes a hundred workshops on psychological safety. One promotion given to someone who delivered results by scorching the earth tells everyone precisely what the real criteria are.

You cannot write your way to a culture. You can only decide, repeatedly and visibly, what you will not accept.

The gap between your stated values and your tolerated behaviours is your actual culture. Everything else is marketing.
