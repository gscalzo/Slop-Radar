Something I've been reflecting on after three years of managing remote teams.

The hardest part was never the coordination. Tools solved that. The hardest part was the loss of ambient information — the things you absorb without anyone deciding to tell you.

Someone sighing at their desk. A conversation you half-overhear. The mood of a room on a Monday.

Remote work made every piece of information intentional. And intentional information is filtered information.

So we started running short unstructured calls with no agenda. Not stand-ups. Just presence. It helped more than any process change we made that year.
