We went fully remote in 2020.

We went back to the office in 2023.

We went fully remote again last month.

Here's what we learned.

Offices don't create culture. Proximity isn't connection. Mandates don't build trust.

What actually worked? Intentionality. Clear async writing. Fewer meetings, better ones. Paying for people to travel and see each other four times a year.

The companies still fighting about days-in-office are asking the wrong question. It was never about location. It was always about whether you trust the people you hired.

Trust scales. Surveillance doesn't.
