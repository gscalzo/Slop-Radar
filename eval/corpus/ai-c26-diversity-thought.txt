Diversity is not a numbers game. It's a systems game.

You can hire your way to a diverse headcount and still have a monoculture, because the question was never who walks through the door. It's whose ideas survive the meeting.

Look at your last ten significant decisions. Who proposed the option that won? Who spoke first? Who got interrupted, and who did the interrupting? Whose objection was treated as a risk to manage and whose was treated as an obstacle to route around?

Representation without influence is decoration.

The organisations getting this right aren't running better recruitment campaigns. They're changing how decisions get made — writing before discussing, rotating who speaks first, making dissent someone's explicit job.

Structure beats good intentions. Every time.
