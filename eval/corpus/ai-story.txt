A junior developer asked me a question last week that completely changed my perspective.

She said: "Why do we do it this way?"

I started to answer. Then I stopped. Because the honest answer was: I don't know. We've always done it this way.

That moment was humbling. Twelve years of experience, and I was defending a process I'd never questioned.

We changed it that afternoon. The new approach saved the team four hours a week.

Never underestimate the power of a beginner's mind. Sometimes the most valuable person in the room is the one who doesn't know the rules yet.
