Unpopular opinion: most LinkedIn advice is written by people who have never done the thing they're advising about.

The "10 lessons from my exit" posts are written by people whose exit was a soft acqui-hire. The hiring threads are written by people who last screened a CV in 2019. The productivity systems are sold by people whose only product is the productivity system.

Here's my test before I take advice from a post: has this person recently done the thing, at the scale I'm operating at, with the constraints I have?

Usually the answer is no. Usually the post is optimised for engagement, not accuracy, because engagement is what the platform pays for.

Read the ones with specifics. Skip the ones with adjectives.
