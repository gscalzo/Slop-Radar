My first manager gave me a task I couldn't do.

I struggled for four days. I was too embarrassed to ask for help — I'd just been hired, and asking felt like admitting they'd made a mistake.

On day five she came over and said: "You've been stuck since Tuesday. Why didn't you say?"

I didn't have an answer.

She said something I've repeated to every person I've managed since: "You have two hours. After two hours of no progress, being stuck is my problem, not yours. Come and find me."

Two hours. A concrete number. Not "reach out any time" — which sounds supportive and helps nobody, because the hard part was never permission. It was knowing when.

Vague invitations to ask for help are not help.
