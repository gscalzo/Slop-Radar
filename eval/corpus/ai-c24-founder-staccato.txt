Year one: I worked 90 hour weeks. We nearly died.

Year two: I hired people smarter than me. We survived.

Year three: I got out of their way. We grew 300%.

The lesson took me three years and almost cost me my marriage.

Your job as a founder isn't to be the best at everything. It's to make yourself unnecessary as fast as possible.

Hire people who scare you slightly. Give them the keys. Get out of the room.

The company you're building should outlive your involvement in every function.

Ego is the most expensive thing on your balance sheet.
