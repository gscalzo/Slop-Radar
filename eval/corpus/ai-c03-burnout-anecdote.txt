Three years ago I collapsed at my desk.

Not metaphorically. An actual ambulance, actual paramedics, actual hospital bed. The diagnosis was exhaustion, which is a polite word for what I'd been doing to myself.

I'd been working 70-hour weeks and calling it dedication. I'd been skipping lunch and calling it focus. I'd been answering Slack at midnight and calling it leadership.

My team didn't need any of that. What they needed was a manager who was present, rested, and capable of making good decisions.

The week I came back, I set a hard stop at 6pm. Revenue didn't fall. Nothing broke. The only thing that changed was that I stopped modelling self-destruction for twelve people who were watching me.

Your team is watching you too.

What example are you setting?
